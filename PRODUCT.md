# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NovelReader serves both everyday web-novel readers and power users who self-host, import Legado-compatible sources, and manage source behavior. It must support focused reading on phones and source browsing or management on larger screens without making either audience work through the other's complexity.

## Product Purpose

NovelReader provides one web-first place to discover books through source-native catalogs, search imported sources, maintain a shelf, and read long-form content. Success means readers can move from discovery or search to a dependable reading session while advanced source behavior remains available, explicit, and safely contained.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, simple, approachable. The product should feel calm and understandable even when the underlying source engine is technical, using direct language and familiar controls rather than exposing implementation concepts unnecessarily.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dense administration console. Avoid configuration-first navigation, cramped tables, jargon-heavy labels, and technical diagnostics dominating ordinary reading workflows. Also avoid decorative product styling that distracts from books or reading.

## Design Principles

1. Put books and reading tasks before source mechanics.
2. Reveal advanced controls where they are relevant instead of making them the default experience.
3. Use familiar platform conventions and plain language so actions remain predictable.
4. Keep source state and failures explicit enough to recover without exposing raw rules or secrets.
5. Treat mobile reading and desktop management as equally supported responsive workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, useful status feedback, and touch-friendly controls during functional development. Formal WCAG conformance and comprehensive visual accessibility auditing are deferred to the final dedicated frontend quality pass, but new work must not knowingly create inaccessible interaction barriers.
